02 — How It Works
01
Understand

We talk through your goals, audience and what the site or app actually needs to do. I ask questions, take notes, and agree on scope before writing a single line of code.

02
Build

I write clean, semantic code — mobile-first, with performance in mind. You get progress updates as sections come together, not one big reveal at the end.

Deploy, check performance and SEO basics, and fix anything that needs polishing. I stick around after launch for the small adjustments that always come up.
